# schemas

- [Error reference schema][sokf:schema-error-reference] - The errors that tramlines reports for one part of a schema or a document, each under the key that tramlines prints.
- [Explanation schema][sokf:schema-explanation] - Why tramlines works the way it does, for a reader who wants to understand one part of tramlines.
- [How-to guide schema][sokf:schema-how-to] - One task with tramlines, shown from start to finish, for a reader who knows what they want to do.
- [Reference schema][sokf:schema-reference] - A page where a reader looks up facts, such as the keys of a schema or the options of the command.
- [Tutorial schema][sokf:schema-tutorial] - A step-by-step lesson in which a reader who is new to tramlines builds one thing that works.
