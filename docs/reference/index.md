# reference

- [The command][sokf:reference-cli] - The subcommands of `tramlines` and their options, the forms of the report, and the exit codes.
- [Errors: kinds of block][sokf:errors-block-kinds] - The errors that tramlines reports for what a block of each kind declares and contains, such as the tag of a code block or the header of a table, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: blocks][sokf:errors-blocks] - The errors that tramlines reports for the blocks of a section, their order, and the number of units in each block, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: the command][sokf:errors-command] - The errors that stop `tramlines validate` before it checks a document, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: examples and guidance][sokf:errors-examples] - The errors that tramlines reports for the examples of a schema, and for guidance that repeats an example.
- [Errors: formats][sokf:errors-formats-and-examples] - The errors that tramlines reports for the line formats of a schema, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: frontmatter][sokf:errors-frontmatter] - The errors that tramlines reports for the frontmatter of a document, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: keys and repairs][sokf:errors-keys-and-repairs] - The errors that tramlines reports for keys and their anchors, and for the text that a repair rewrites to match the frontmatter or the schema.
- [Errors: markers][sokf:errors-keys-markers-scaffolds] - The errors that tramlines reports for the markers of a scaffold that a document keeps.
- [Errors: lifecycle and ids][sokf:errors-lifecycle-and-ids] - The errors that tramlines reports for the lifecycle of a schema, for the rules that it gates, and for the id template.
- [Errors: lines and pairings][sokf:errors-lines-and-pairings] - The errors that tramlines reports for the lines of a code block and for rules that pair one unit with each key of another rule, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: lines and length][sokf:errors-lines] - The errors that tramlines reports for a line that fails its shape or a ban, and for a document that is too long.
- [Errors: sections inside sections][sokf:errors-nested-sections] - The errors that tramlines reports for a section that declares a parent section, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: scaffolds][sokf:errors-scaffolds] - The errors that tramlines reports for the scaffold that a new document starts from, when a schema can't derive it or the scaffold fails the schema.
- [Errors: shapes and templates][sokf:errors-schema-shapes] - The errors that tramlines reports for the shapes of the rules of a schema, and for the holes of its templates.
- [Errors: schema types and keys][sokf:errors-schema-types-keys-lifecycle] - The errors that tramlines reports for the types, the keys, the key derivations, and the counts of a schema, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: the schema][sokf:errors-schema] - The errors that tramlines reports for the YAML, the keys, the values and the regular expressions of a schema file, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: headings and sections][sokf:errors-sections] - The errors that tramlines reports for the headings of a document and the sections they open, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors: units][sokf:errors-units] - The errors that tramlines reports for the paragraphs, items, and rows that the rules of a block select and count, and how to fix each one.
- [Errors][sokf:reference-errors] - How to read an error from tramlines, and where each error is explained.
- [Schema keys][sokf:reference-schema-keys] - Every key that a markdown schema can use, by the part of the schema that it belongs to.
- [Built-in types][sokf:reference-types] - The types that a hole of a template can have, how a schema or a formats file defines its own, and the order in which a name is looked up.
